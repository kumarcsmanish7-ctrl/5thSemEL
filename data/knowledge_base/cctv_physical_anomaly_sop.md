# Standard Operating Procedure: CCTV Video Anomaly & Physical Security Incident Response
**Document ID:** SOP-SEC-VID-002  
**Classification:** Physical Security & AI Analytics Response  
**Applicability:** Video Analytics Pipeline, YOLO/ConvLSTM Anomaly Detectors, On-site Patrol

## 1. Video Anomaly Classifications
AI-driven CCTV analysis flags frames where reconstruction error exceeds threshold ($E > \tau$) or where YOLO object tracking detects restricted events:
- **Unauthorized Perimeter Intrusion:** Human or unauthorized vehicle detected inside restricted or after-hours zones.
- **Loitering & Suspicious Stagnation:** Static human presence detected near secure perimeters or high-value infrastructure exceeding 120 seconds.
- **Camera Tampering / Occlusion:** Abrupt luminance drop, total frame occlusion (lens spray/covering), or severe optical shift indicating physical tampering.
- **Unusual Motion Dynamics:** Rapid erratic movement, fleeing persons, or physical altercations.

## 2. Verification & Escalation Procedures
1. **Automated Alert Triage:**
   - Review the anomaly score generated by the ConvLSTM / YOLO pipeline.
   - Scores between 0.60 – 0.79 classify as **Medium Severity** (warrants operator visual check).
   - Scores $\ge 0.80$ classify as **High / Critical Severity** (triggers immediate tactical response).

2. **Operator Action Checklist:**
   - **Step 1:** Access the Live Dashboard frame preview and review the 10-second pre-event buffer.
   - **Step 2:** Correlate with physical access control logs (badge swipes, gate sensors) within the zone.
   - **Step 3:** For confirmed unauthorized entry, initiate audible warning or spotlight deterrents if remotely equipped.
   - **Step 4:** Dispatch physical security personnel to coordinates identified by the camera location ID.

3. **Camera Health & Anti-Tampering Protocol:**
   - If frame loss coincides with high reconstruction error, inspect both the physical camera mount and network port status.
   - Verify camera feed integrity to rule out man-in-the-middle video injection or physical obstruction.
